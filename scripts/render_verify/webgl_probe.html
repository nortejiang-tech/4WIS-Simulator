Two coplanar quads (blue behind, magenta in front) drawn 2 cm apart at ~12 m — the vehicle's actual cabin-on-shell seam scale — through the app's near:0.1 / far:2000 range. Solid magenta = the depth buffer resolves the seam (24-bit, e.g. this Mac). Speckling / shimmer of blue through magenta = z-fighting, which appears once DEPTH_BITS ≤ 16 (software / older Windows GPU fallbacks). Combined with a low FPS, this is the flicker + jitter Windows users report.
